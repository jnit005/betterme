Frequently asked questions

What to know before you begin practising with BetterMe.

What is BetterMe?

BetterMe is an interview-practice platform designed to help students and professionals rehearse before real interviews, reflect on their answers, and build confidence through practice.

Who can use BetterMe?

Students, job seekers, career changers, and working professionals can explore the Engineering, Sales & Marketing, and Government Exam pathways.

Is the first interview session free?

Yes. Your first practice session is free and includes up to five questions.

Do I need an account to explore?

No. You can browse the platform and pathways without signing in. You’ll be asked to sign in or create an account when you launch an interview.

How does a practice session work?

Choose a pathway, complete the setup form, and start a question-based practice session. At this stage, answers are not recorded: voice recording, automated evaluation, and downloadable reports are planned for later development.

Will BetterMe guarantee that I get a job?

No platform can guarantee a job offer. BetterMe is intended to give you a place to prepare and practise before an actual interview.

Can I use BetterMe on my phone?

The site is designed to adapt to smaller screens. For the most comfortable practice experience, use a modern browser and a stable connection.
